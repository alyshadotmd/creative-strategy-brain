# References

## Inspiration

What sparked this concept or shows the execution we are aiming at. One entry per reference: what it is, where it came from, and the specific thing we are borrowing — a structure, a pacing choice, an opening move. A reference with no note attached is a mood board, not a brief input.

## Media used

Paths into `assets/`. Never copies of the files themselves.

- Footage: `assets/footage/<shoot-or-creator>/`
- Prior cuts being reused as source: `assets/ads/<ad-name>/`
- Product or lifestyle stills: `assets/images/`
