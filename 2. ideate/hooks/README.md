# Hooks

The swipe file `creative-engine` reads at Step 8, and where `review-audit` sends standout customer language. A hook is the opening expression of an offer — the words that stop the scroll and the frames that earn the next second.

## What goes here

Two kinds of file:

- **`openers-spoken.md`** — lines that work said aloud: video, UGC, talking head. Written the way people actually speak.
- **`openers-written.md`** — lines that work on screen: statics, headlines, first-frame text overlays.

Plus **`customer-language.md`** — verbatim phrases pulled from reviews by `review-audit` (Bucket 7), tagged with product, bucket, and persona. These are the raw material; many are hooks with zero editing.

Each entry records: the line itself, the tactic it uses (one plain phrase), which awareness stage it fits, which persona or trigger moment it came from or suits, and — once it has run — how it performed.

## Rules

- Quote customers verbatim. Never polish a customer's words into marketing.
- A hook saved without a stage, a persona, or a trigger moment is a clever line, not usable material yet — name them when you save it.
- Anything that gets quoted back by customers in comments or reviews goes in here.
