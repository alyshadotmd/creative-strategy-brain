# Focus

What the work is pointed at right now. The first thing to read before opening a concept, and the thing to check a concept against before briefing it.

Not strategy in the broad sense — positioning, audience and messaging live in `brand/` and `1. research/`. This is narrower and more perishable: what matters *this stretch*, and what is deliberately being left alone to make room for it.

## The objective

One sentence. The thing the business is trying to move, stated so it could be wrong. A number where there is one.

## Why now

What changed that makes this the objective rather than the one before it — a launch, a season, a competitor move, a result that came back. An objective with no "why now" tends to be last quarter's, still running.

## The constraint

What limits the work: budget, production capacity, a creator roster, a timeline, a compliance review. Name it plainly. Most concepts die on a constraint nobody wrote down.

## Not doing

The list that makes this a focus rather than a wish. Products, audiences, channels and formats deliberately out of scope for now — and, where it helps, the one-line reason each is parked.

A focus with nothing in this section is not a focus.

## How we'll know

What tells us this worked, and roughly when. The metric, the threshold if there is one, and where the number comes from.

## Open

Decisions still outstanding that would change the above if they landed. Kept here so a concept built against an unsettled assumption is visible as a risk rather than a surprise.
