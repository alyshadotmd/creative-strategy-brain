# QA — pre-launch

Reviewed against `script.md`, `brief.md`, and the brand's claims list. This gates the launch; the post-launch teardown lives in `4. analyze`.

**Reviewed:** <date> · **Delivery:** path in `assets/`

## Did they do what we asked

Against the brief, point by point. Where the delivery departs from it, say whether the departure improves the ad or breaks it — a producer's good instinct is not a failure, and a missed instruction that nobody noticed is a brief problem, not only a producer problem.

## Claims

Every claim made in the delivery, checked against what the brand can say. Anything not on the permitted list is blocking.

## Brand

Against the brand kit — marks, type, color, tone.

## Does it work

Does the hook earn the next second, does it read in the feed at actual size with sound off, does the close ask clearly.

## Verdict

Approved · approved with fixes · back to the producer

## Feedback

What to change, specific enough to act on. Each note says what is wrong, why it matters, and what would fix it.
